# Controlled Customization  |  Generative AI on Vertex AI  |  Google Cloud ## What's next ### Console 1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Vertex AI \> Media Studio** page. 2. In the lower task panel, click edit **Edit image** . 3. Click **Upload** to select your locally-stored image to edit. 4. In the **Mode** section of the **Parameters** panel, click **Control** . 5. Optional. Modify the **Number of results** , provide a **Negative prompt** , or modify the **Advanced options \> Region** . 6. In the **Advanced options** section, select the **Guide image type** : **Standard RGB** , **Canny edge** , or **Scribble** . 7. In the **Advanced options** section, select the **Control condition** : **Canny edge** or **Scribble** . 8. In the prompt field ( *Write your prompt...* ), provide a text prompt. 9. Click play\_arrow **Generate** .
